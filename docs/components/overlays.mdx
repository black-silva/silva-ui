# Overlays and compound components

These components provide structure, styling, ARIA roles, and state data attributes. They are deliberately **stateless**: your application owns interaction, focus management, keyboard handling, and portal behaviour.

For production modal behaviour, add your own focus trap, Escape handling, background inertness, and portal strategy around the visual primitives.

## Tabs

Tabs are stateless. Keep the selected value in application state, set `data-active` on the current tab, and hide inactive panels.

<Tabs sync={false}>
  <Tab title="Preview">

<Frame>

</Frame>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Code">

```tsx
<Tabs value={activeTab}>
  <TabList aria-label="Account settings">
    <Tab value="profile" data-active={activeTab === "profile"} onClick={() => setActiveTab("profile")}>Profile</Tab>
    <Tab value="security" data-active={activeTab === "security"} onClick={() => setActiveTab("security")}>Security</Tab>
  </TabList>
  <TabPanel value="profile" hidden={activeTab !== "profile"}>Profile content</TabPanel>
  <TabPanel value="security" hidden={activeTab !== "security"}>Security content</TabPanel>
</Tabs>
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Dialog

`Dialog` provides modal structure and `data-state`; your app controls `open`, focus management, Escape handling, background inertness, and portal placement.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Code">

```tsx
<Dialog open={isOpen}>
  <DialogOverlay />
  <DialogContent>
    <DialogHeader>
      <DialogTitle>Delete project?</DialogTitle>
      <DialogDescription>This cannot be undone.</DialogDescription>
    </DialogHeader>
    <DialogFooter>
      <DialogClose onClick={() => setIsOpen(false)}>Cancel</DialogClose>
      <Button onClick={removeProject}>Delete</Button>
    </DialogFooter>
  </DialogContent>
```

## Popover

`Popover` composes a trigger with a positioned content panel. Drive `data-state` and dismiss behaviour from your application.

## Accordion

`Accordion` is stateless. Apply `data-state="open" | "closed"` to each item, trigger, and content from your own state.

<Tabs sync={false}>
  <Tab title="Preview">

<Frame>
  <iframe src="https://black-silva.github.io/silva-ui/previews/?example=overlay-10" title="Rendered Accordion preview" className="w-full h-80 rounded-xl"></iframe>
</Frame>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Code">

```tsx
<Accordion type="single">
  <AccordionItem value="shipping" data-state={openItem === "shipping" ? "open" : "closed"}>
    <AccordionTrigger onClick={() => setOpenItem("shipping")} data-state={openItem === "shipping" ? "open" : "closed"}>
      When does my order ship?
    </AccordionTrigger>
    <AccordionContent data-state={openItem === "shipping" ? "open" : "closed"}>
      Orders leave our warehouse in one to two business days.
    </AccordionContent>
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
```

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
